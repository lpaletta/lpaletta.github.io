Overview

This page presents illustrative animations of the 2D signal-rule decoders introduced in Local decoding of the toric code under high noise, L. Paletta (2026).

The rule dynamically mediates an effective attractive interaction between defects, leading to their local pairing and annihilation.

Local State Space

Each lattice site stores the local stabilizer measurement outcome (a −1 outcome is referred to as a defect) together with:

  • 12 binary variables: 1- and 2-forward-signals, 1- and 2-anti-signals, each subdivided by cardinal direction.
  • 6 integer-valued stacks: 1- and 2-stacks, subdivided by cardinal direction.

Elementary Update Mechanisms

  1. Matching. Neighboring defects are paired by applying a local Pauli correction (i).
  2. 1-forward dynamics.
    1. Creation: 1-forward-signals are emitted from defect sites; the associated 1-stacks are incremented by 1 (ii).
    2. Propagation: 1-forward-signals move one lattice step (viii).
  3. 2-forward dynamics.
    1. Creation: 2-forward-signals are generated from 1-forward-signals and defect sites; the associated stacks are incremented by 1 (iii).
    2. Propagation: 2-forward-signals move one lattice step (viii).
  4. Attraction. Defects encountering 1- or 2-forward-signals trigger a local Pauli correction (iv).
  5. Anti-signal dynamics.
    1. Creation: 1-anti-signals (resp. 2-anti-signals) are triggered when 1-stacks (resp. 2-stacks) are decremented in the absence of a defect (v), (vi).
    2. Propagation and recombination: (repeated three times) anti-signals propagate one lattice step (viii) and recombine with forward-signals of the same type and direction (vii).

Visualization Conventions

Printing priority:

  • defect > 1-forward-signal > 2-forward-signal
  • 1-stack > 2-stack > 1-anti-signal > 2-anti-signal

Additional rules:

  • Multiple signals of the same type on one site are represented by a square.
  • The maximum value of the local stack is displayed in white.
