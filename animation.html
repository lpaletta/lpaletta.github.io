This page presents illustrative animations of the 2D signal-rule decoders introduced in Local decoder for the toric code via signal exchange, L. Paletta (2026).

Layout

Each lattice site stores:

  • Defect (−1 stabilizer outcome)
  • 16 binary variables: 1- and 2-forward-signals, 1- and 2-anti-signals (by cardinal direction)
  • 8 integer variables: 1- and 2-stacks (by cardinal direction)

The rule dynamically mediates an effective attractive interaction between defects via the propagation of 1- and 2-forward-signals. Stack and anti-signal dynamics erase forward-signals at the end, ensuring the decoder eventually returns to the zero configuration.

Interactive simulation

Run the decoder step by step. The step j of iteration i is indicated as i.j (the anti-signal sub-steps are all labelled 5). Arrow keys step through the dynamics, space plays or pauses.

 

Elementary Update Mechanisms

  1. Matching of neighboring defects
  2. Creation of 1-forward-signals
  3. Propagation of 1-forward-signals
  4. Creation of 2-forward-signals
  5. Propagation of 2-forward-signals
  6. Attraction of defects
  7. Creation of anti-signals
  8. Propagation & recombination of anti-signals
